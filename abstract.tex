\begin{abstract}
This proposal introduces a deterministic computational framework to upscale low--Reynolds--number particle mechanics into macro--scale environmental transport equations. By coupling open--source Stokesian Dynamics with high--order Finite--Difference advection--diffusion--reaction solvers, we eliminate empirical black--box parameters and provide a mechanistic baseline for particle trapping along the complex benthic interfaces of Tayrona National Natural Park.
\end{abstract}
