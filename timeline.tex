\section{Project Timeline and Key Milestones}
\label{sec:timeline}

To ensure accountability and mitigate logistical bottlenecks, the proposed 24--month development schedule utilizes a concurrent, pipelined engineering workflow. Rather than processing phases sequentially, data--driven dependencies overlap; early--stage micro--scale simulation outputs are immediately leveraged to develop and debug the macro--scale continuum solvers while long--term production runs continue to execute.

\subsection{Milestone Descriptions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Milestone 1 (Month 06):} Successful compilation and verification of the low--Reynolds Stokesian configuration files with custom boundary geometry arrays on the UPR HPC infrastructure.
    \item \textbf{Milestone 2 (Month 16):} Completion of the full discrete simulation database tracking multi--body suspension trajectories across the entire targeted parameter space of local shear and salinity profiles.
    \item \textbf{Milestone 3 (Month 20):} Complete integration of the mathematical upscaling protocol, generating verified effective dispersion tensors ($\bm{D}_{\text{eff}}$) and continuous kinetic trapping rates ($R$) free of \emph{ad hoc} constants.
    \item \textbf{Milestone 4 (Month 24):} Public release of the validated open--source computational code repository alongside dissemination of findings in high--impact transport phenomena and computational mechanics journals.
\end{itemize}
